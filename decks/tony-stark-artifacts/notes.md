# Tony Stark artifacts — decision log

Silly, for-fun brew (not part of the commander cycle). Owner lightened the rules: bigger budget,
Game Changers allowed **only if genuinely theme-related**, still no non-games / no Day-Night.

## Core idea
Use the **Tony Stark** face only (never pay {4}{U}{R} to flip to Iron Man). His
`{1},{T}: look at top 4, take an artifact` is a repeatable impulse-dig. **Untap or copy** Tony to
run it several times a turn -> flood the board with artifacts. Colour identity is Izzet (U/R) even
though the front is mono-U.

## Pillars
- **Untap / reuse** (single-target): Freed from the Real, Pemmin's Aura (`{U}: untap`, near-infinite
  digs with a mana rock), Thousand-Year Elixir (also gives Tony haste T-drop), Magewright's Stone,
  Umbral Mantle.
- **Copy the activation**: Rings of Brighthearth, Illusionist's Bracers -> dig twice per tap.
- **Mass untap**: Unwinding Clock (all artifacts each turn — free Shao Jun fuel on any turn),
  Dramatic Reversal (bursts Tony + rocks; the accidental-combo owner blessed), Intruder Alarm
  (every token ETB untaps Tony while going wide).
- **Token-variety sub-theme** (light "one of each"): Treasure (Storm-Kiln, Captain Lannery, Xorn),
  Thopter (Sai, Efficient Construction, Whirler Rogue, Pia Nalaar, Thopter Spy Network), Servo
  (Saheeli, Retrofitter Foundry), Powerstone (Urza, Geology Enthusiast, The Mana Rig), Blood
  (Voldaren Epicure), and **Academy Manufactor** makes Clue+Food+Treasure at once. Variety matters
  for Brudiclad (pick the best copy target) and Depthshaker (bodies to animate).
- **Cost reducers** so a dug artifact still leaves {U} to re-dig: Foundry Inspector, Etherium
  Sculptor, Cloud Key, Enthusiastic Mechanaut, Artificer Class.

## Closers (all cheap, none are Game Changers)
- **Brudiclad, Telchor Engineer** — tokens have haste; at combat make a Myr then turn the whole
  swarm into copies of one fatty -> hasty alpha strike.
- **Shao Jun** — `tap two artifacts: 1 to each opponent`; with untappers + a wide artifact board it
  becomes a repeatable drain.
- **Graaz, Unstoppable Juggernaut** — whole board becomes 5/3 must-attack Juggernauts, go-wide finish.
- (Bonus jank) **Hellkite Tyrant** — steal-all-artifacts alt win / punish artifact decks.
- CUT: **Depthshaker Titan** — owner kept only Graaz as the go-wide payoff (jankier). Depthshaker
  parked in CONSIDER.md if we ever want the animate-artifacts alpha back.

## Token-variety sub-theme (middle-leaning-in — owner wanted several new *types*)
Types now covered, one-of-each flavor that feeds Brudiclad (best copy target) + Graaz (go-wide):
- Treasure (Storm-Kiln, Xorn), Thopter (Sai, Efficient Construction, Whirler Rogue, Pia Nalaar,
  Thopter Spy Network), Servo (Saheeli, Retrofitter Foundry), Powerstone (Urza, The Mana Rig),
  Blood (Voldaren Epicure), Clue+Food+Treasure at once (Academy Manufactor).
- NEW types added this pass:
  - **Weapons Manufacturing** -> *Munitions* (owner-requested): every nontoken artifact ETB makes a
    Munitions token that deals 2 when it leaves. Damage engine + more artifact bodies.
  - **Chrome Host Seedshark** -> *Incubator* off our many noncreature/artifact casts; evasive.
  - **Maskwood Nexus** -> *Shapeshifter/changeling* tokens; also makes all our tokens every type
    (ideal Brudiclad copy target).
  - **Toggo, Goblin Weaponsmith** -> *Rock* Equipment tokens on landfall + sac-for-2 reach (35 lands).
  - **Prototype Portal** -> flexible repeatable *copy* tokens of any imprinted artifact.
  - **Worldwalker Helm** -> rider: every artifact token we make ALSO makes a *Map* token; plus
    `{1}{U},{T}: copy target artifact token` (repeatable go-wide copier). Very on-theme, repeated.
  - **Goblin Kaboomist** -> *Land Mine* token each upkeep (repeated; coin-flip self-ping is the jank).
  - **Ominous Seas** -> accrues a foreshadow counter on every draw; 8 -> an 8/8 *Kraken* (great
    Brudiclad copy-target). Repeated build-up off our heavy card draw.
  - **Donatello, the Brains** -> rider: any token we'd create is made PLUS a *Mutagen* token
    (TMNT set). Repeated, on-theme; Mutagen sacs for a +1/+1 counter (buff a Brudiclad target/attacker).

## Extra jank wincon
- **Concert Kaboomist** ({1}{R}{R}) — ETB / turned-face-up deals X to an opponent = noncreature spells
  cast since your last turn. We cast a pile of cheap artifacts, so X balloons; **Disguise {R}** lets you
  flash it down and unmorph for a SECOND trigger. Lone Goblin (not a goblin-swarm payoff), so it
  respects the "goblins are their own archetype" rule.
- CUTS for these two: **Prototype Portal** (clunky, needs an imprint to come online; copies covered by
  Worldwalker Helm + Saheeli), **Rapid Hybridization** (redundant with Pongify).
- CUTS to make room (redundant / off-theme / ETB-only per owner's "repeated > ETB" lean):
  Captain Lannery Storm (3rd treasure), Geology Enthusiast (3rd powerstone), Mind's Eye (slow draw),
  Guardian Idol (trimmed 1 of 9 rocks), **Maskwood Nexus** (doesn't actually help Brudiclad — its own
  token is a vanilla 2/2 and it doesn't animate our noncreature tokens), **Reckoner Bankbuster**
  (generic repeatable draw, Vehicle/Pilot payoff not artifact-synergy driven), **Pia Nalaar**
  (ETB one-shot Thopter; kept the repeatable Thopter makers instead).

## Owner preferences captured this pass
- Slightly prefer REPEATED token effects over one-shot ETB makers — bias future adds that way.
- Likes stacking token-creation RIDERS ("more triggers" is fun). Current UR-legal rider stack:
  Xorn (+Treasure), Worldwalker Helm (+Map, artifact tokens), Stridehangar Automaton (+Thopter,
  artifact tokens; also Thopters +1/+1), Donatello (+Mutagen, any token), Academy Manufactor
  (Clue/Food/Treasure -> one of each). One token-creation event can cascade into 4-5 tokens; these
  are replacement effects that each apply ONCE, so it's finite (no infinite loop) but very silly.
  Peregrin Took (LOTR Food rider) is mono-green -> can't run. Don & Raph benched (affinity attacker,
  not a rider).
- The "artifact-token activation cost {1} less" effect the owner wanted is ALREADY covered by
  **Forensic Gadgeteer** (activated abilities of our artifacts, incl. token sac costs, cost {1} less;
  the only other printing, Mutagen Man, is mono-green).
- Goblins are their own archetype -> stay away from goblin-swarm payoffs (Krenko et al.) even though
  they'd be nasty with Graaz. (Lone goblins like Concert Kaboomist / Goblin Kaboomist are fine.)
- No Gold/"Mutagen"... Gold: only UR source is Curse of Opulence (attack-triggered, political) -> skip.

## Manabase
35 lands, ~7 rocks. Pip demand U 63% / R 37% (`analyze` [c], 52 pips). Counting every source by color:
- **U sources = 29**: 11 UR duals (Command Tower, Steam Vents, Spirebluff Canal, Shivan Reef, Sulfur
  Falls, Riverglide Pathway, Cascade Bluffs, Frostboil Snarl, Prismari Campus, Fiery Islet, Spire of
  Industry) + 12 Island + Halimar Depths + Mystic Sanctuary + 4 dual rocks (Arcane Signet, Izzet
  Signet, Talisman of Creativity, Prismatic Lens).
- **R sources = 21**: same 11 duals + 6 Mountain + the same 4 dual rocks.
- Karsten (~10 single-pip, ~13-14 double-pip): double-U (Pemmin's, Whirler Rogue, Thopter Spy Network)
  have 29 U; double-R (Concert Kaboomist {1}{R}{R}) has 21 R. Both clear with margin.
- Supply skews U58/R42 vs demand U63/R37 -> slightly red-rich, which is fine insurance for the double-R
  Kaboomist + early red interaction while blue is over-served anyway. No change needed.
- 4 colorless-only lands (Reliquary Tower, Inventors' Fair, Academy Ruins, Mishra's Factory) are all
  high-utility; rock fixing covers the colored gap. ~5 taplands, acceptable for a low-curve 2-color deck.
