# Consider next — The Wandering Minstrel

Running list of cards/ideas to weigh on the next pass. Seeded from an EDHREC
reconciliation (2026-09) + owner notes. Nothing here is committed to the list;
it's a shortlist to evaluate against current cuts.

## Towns that earn a home "in the theme"
The Minstrel makes lands enter untapped, so the usual enters-tapped downside on
Towns is cancelled, AND every extra Town raises the commander's pump count (the
Town payoff is our finisher, not just utility). We passed on generic Towns
before, but these specifically reward *owning Towns* via their oracle text —
give them a home when we reconsider:

- **Town Greeter** — {1}{G}, ~$0.22. ETB mill 4, grab a land; gain 2 life if it's a Town. Enabler + fixing.
- **Qiqirn Merchant** — {2}{U}, ~$0.09. Rummage; sac-draw-3 ability costs {1} less per Town. Payoff.
- **PuPu UFO** — {2}, ~$0.65. Flyer, taps to drop a land from hand; base power = number of Towns. Payoff + ramp.

## FF landfall / go-wide token cards (close-enough power, on both sub-themes)
Land go-wide + landfall is our core; these fit even if not perfectly optimal:
- **Sazh's Chocobo** — {G}, ~$1.26. Landfall: +1/+1 counter on itself. Cheap growing 1-drop.
- **Chocobo Racetrack** — {3}{G}{G}, ~$0.43. Landfall → 2/2 green Bird token (self-pumping). Best go-wide+landfall fit.
- **Call the Mountain Chocobo** — {3}{R}, ~$0.12. Mountain tutor + a landfall Bird token; has flashback.
- **Summon: Fat Chocobo** — {4}{G}, ~$0.22. Saga: landfall Bird token, then team trample.
- **Gysahl Greens** — {1}{G}, ~$0.16. Landfall Bird-token maker (cheap enabler).
- **Sazh Katzroy** — {3}{G}, ~$0.61. Bird/basic-land tutor + counter doubler on attack.
- **Traveling Chocobo** — {2}{G}, ~$18 (PROXY CANDIDATE). Play lands/Birds off top + doubles land/Bird ETB triggers. Strong landfall payoff, chase-priced.
