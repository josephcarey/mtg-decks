# Thantis — CONSIDER (running shortlist; nothing here is in the list yet)

## Cheap subs for the 5 flagged proxies (drops the deck to ~$55-60)
- **Karazikar, the Eye Tyrant** (~$13, goad payoff + draw) → **Vengeful Ancestor** / **Grismold** is
  already in; cheap goad-payoff sub: **Frontier Warmonger** or just lean on Kardur + Marchesa's Decree.
- **No Mercy** (~$10, kills anything that hits you) → **AE­ther Flash** hurts our tokens; better cheap
  punisher: **Caltrops is pricey too** — use **Powerstone Shard? no**; simplest sub is **Dread** (forces
  attacks + you choose) or another copy of the fog plan. Cheapest true "attack-me-and-die": **Cage of
  Hands? white**. Realistically leave the slot as a 2nd wrath (**Bontu's Last Reckoning**, cheap).
- **Arachnogenesis** (~$8) → **Tangle** (~$4, all attackers don't untap) or **Spike Weaver** as a
  repeatable fog body; cheapest is plain **Constant Mists**/**Moment's Peace** substitutes.
- **Ohran Frostfang** (~$7, deathtouch anthem + draw-on-attack) → **Ohran Viper** already in; add
  **Bow of Nylea** (~cheap, grants deathtouch) or **Viridian Longbow**.
- **Toski, Bearer of Secrets** (~$6, draw on attack, hard to remove) → **Coastal Piracy**-style is
  blue; stay in colors with **Reconnaissance Mission? blue**. Cheapest in-Jund: lean on
  **Deathreap Ritual** (already in) + **Return of the Wildspeaker**.

## More group-slug / punisher (probably want 1-2 more)
- **Karazikar** (already listed as proxy). **Vengeful Ancestor** — repeatable goad + damage.
- **Rite of the Raging Storm** (IN) — symmetric forced-attack tokens; great engine.
- **Vicious Shadows** (~pricey) — huge payoff when creatures die in all the forced combat.
- **Warstorm Surge / Where Ancients Tread** (both IN) — ETB burn as bodies land.
- **Disrupt Decorum** (IN) — one-shot goad-the-table.

## Deathtouch / reach depth (if walls feel thin)
- **Bow of Nylea**, **Gorgon's Head**, **Basilisk Collar** (deathtouch equipment).
- **Nemata, Grove Guardian**, **Ishkanah** (IN), **Spider Spawning** (token fog wall).
- **Watcher for Tomorrow / Silklash Spider** (IN) — reach + wipe fliers.

## Overrun / finisher options (currently Overrun, Overwhelming Stampede, Wrecking Ogre, Vigor)
- **Pathbreaker Ibex** (~$15, chase) — best overrun body but pricey; skip for budget.
- **End-Raze Foragers**, **Kamahl's Will**, **Beastmaster Ascension** — cheaper mass-pump.
- **Xenagos, God of Revels** (~$10) — doubles power + haste on a forced attacker; upgrade tier.

  the plan is grind, not tempo bursts. Leaving it out; note here in case we want e.g. **Seize the Day**
