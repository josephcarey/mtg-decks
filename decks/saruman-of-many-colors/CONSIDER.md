# Consider next — Saruman of Many Colors

Running list of cards/ideas to weigh on the next pass. Seeded from an EDHREC
reconciliation (2026-09). Nothing here is committed to the list.

## Theme reminder
Spellslinger + theft/copy + LOTR villains. **Mill is only the copy-trigger
side effect, NOT a wincon.** EDHREC's #1 theme for Saruman is mill (584 decks),
so its root-page misses (Consuming Aberration, Ruin Crab, Maddening Cacophony)
are the mill deck we intentionally didn't build — correctly skipped.

## Better scouting lens
Use the theme subpages, not the root page:
`deck edhrec saruman-of-many-colors --theme theft` (or `--theme control`).

## Candidate adds (on sub-theme: theft / control)
- **Breach the Multiverse** — theft/reanimator finisher (~$10.87, PROXY CANDIDATE).
- **Drown in the Loch** — flexible counter/removal, scales with graveyards.
- **Lotho, Corrupt Shirriff** — villain flavor + tax/treasure.

## Deliberately NOT chasing
- Dedicated mill payoffs (Consuming Aberration, Ruin Crab, Maddening
  Cacophony) — off-plan; mill here is incidental.
