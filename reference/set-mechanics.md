# Set Mechanics Reference

Evergreen notes on recent mechanics relevant to the decks in this workspace. Verify exact
wording against Scryfall before relying on any interaction.

## Waterbend (Avatar: The Last Airbender)
Waterbend is effectively a **fusion of Convoke and Improvise**. To help pay a spell or
ability with the Waterbend keyword, you may **tap untapped creatures and/or artifacts you
control**, and each one tapped this way pays for **{1}** (one generic mana). Like Convoke,
it lets a wide board of creatures "chip in"; like Improvise, artifacts can pitch in too.
This rewards go-wide token boards — a tapped-out swarm can still fund a big Waterbend spell.

## Earthbend
Earthbend is a **+1/+1 counter** mechanic: "Earthbend N" puts N +1/+1 counters on a target
land or creature you control (turning lands into creatures / growing your board). It plays
well with anything that cares about counters or about creature-lands, and it dovetails with
landfall shells that already field many lands.

## Final Fantasy "Towns"
The Final Fantasy set introduced **Towns** — a land subtype cycle of **23 Towns**. Highlights:
- A **dual-Town color-pair cycle**: each of these taps for one of two colors, functioning as
  budget dual lands.
- **Any-color Towns** — e.g. **Capital City**, **Crossroads Village**, and **Starting Town** —
  tap for any color.
- **Most Towns enter tapped**, which is normally the cost of their fixing. **The Wandering
  Minstrel neutralizes that drawback** ("lands you control enter untapped"), so a Towns-heavy
  manabase comes online at full speed under the commander.
- Beyond fixing, Towns **fuel The Wandering Minstrel's scaling pump**: the more Towns you
  control, the bigger the pump, turning the manabase itself into a repeatable finisher.
